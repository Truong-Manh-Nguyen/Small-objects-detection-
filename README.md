# Small-objects-detection-
This research investigated AI-based detection of waste littering, with a focus on cigarette butts and other small trash. I evaluated models on two distinct datasets: (i) a littering dataset in which most trash is extremely small, and (ii) a set of videos of people smoking where the cigarette must be detected. Prior work by other staff using state-of-the-art YOLO variants achieved 46.7% accuracy on the littering dataset and 21% on the cigarette dataset. I replaced these with RF-DETR, a recent DETR-family model designed for real-time detection and obtain ~90% accuracy on the littering dataset and 77.8% accuracy on the cigarette dataset. These results indicate substantial headroom for further improvement and operational deployment.

Because the information related to images and videos used for training and testing must be kept confidential, I cannot disclose this information along with the video results after applying the model and detection.
